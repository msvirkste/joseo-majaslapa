FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much do accounting services cost?

The cost is determined individually, depending on the size of the company, the volume of documents, and the range of services required. The first consultation is free — during it, I assess the workload and prepare a specific price quote.

Do I need to visit the office in person?

No — cooperation takes place entirely remotely. I work with clients across Latvia regardless of location; in-person meetings are possible if desired, but not required.

Can you take over accounting from another accountant or firm?

Yes, I regularly take over accounting from other service providers. The process begins with a review of the existing documentation, so the transition happens safely and without disrupting your company's daily operations.

How does document and information exchange work?

Entirely digital — documents can be submitted by email or via a convenient cloud service, whichever suits the client best. Communication takes place by phone, email, or video call.

What kind of companies are your services suited for?

I specialize in accounting for Latvian limited liability companies (SIA) and sole proprietors — small and medium-sized businesses that need reliable, personalized bookkeeping without the bureaucracy of a large service provider.
